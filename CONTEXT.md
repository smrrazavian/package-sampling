# Package Sampling

A Python port of the R **sampling** package (Tillé & Matei): unequal-probability
sampling designs for finite populations, where every unit may carry its own
probability of entering the sample.

## Language

### Sampling

**Design**:
A rule for drawing a sample from a finite population, characterised by the
inclusion probabilities it realises. A design is built from a target `pik` and
can then be drawn from repeatedly.
_Avoid_: method, algorithm, sampler, scheme

**Inclusion probability (π)**:
The probability that a given unit enters the sample. Written `pik` in code, one
value per population unit.
_Avoid_: weight, selection probability, propensity

**Joint inclusion probability (π₂)**:
The probability that a given *pair* of units both enter the sample. Forms an
N×N matrix whose diagonal is π.
_Avoid_: second-order probability, pairwise probability, pi2 matrix

**Live unit**:
A unit whose π lies strictly inside the open interval `(eps, 1-eps)`, and whose
membership is therefore decided at random.
_Avoid_: active unit, eligible unit, candidate

**Deterministic unit**:
A unit whose π sits at or beyond the ends of that interval, so its membership is
settled before any draw: always in, or never in.
_Avoid_: fixed unit, certain unit, forced unit

**Fixed-size design**:
A design whose realised sample size is the same on every draw. Poisson is the
one design here that is not fixed-size.
_Avoid_: fixed-n design, constant-size design

**Entropy**:
The measure a maximum-entropy design maximises — subject to matching the target
π and holding the sample size fixed, it spreads probability across possible
samples as evenly as possible.

### Estimation

**Horvitz–Thompson estimator**:
The design-unbiased estimator of a population total, formed by weighting each
sampled unit's value by the reciprocal of its π.
_Avoid_: HT estimator (in prose), inverse-probability estimator

**Sen–Yates–Grundy variance**:
The unbiased variance of the Horvitz–Thompson estimator under a fixed-size
design, expressed in terms of π and π₂.
_Avoid_: SYG (in prose), Yates–Grundy variance

**Yates–Grundy condition**:
The property `π_kl ≤ π_k π_l` for distinct units, which guarantees the
Sen–Yates–Grundy variance is non-negative. Systematic sampling does not satisfy
it; that is a property of the design, not a defect.

**Hájek estimator**:
A ratio-form alternative to Horvitz–Thompson that divides by the estimated
population size rather than the known one.

### Parity with R

**Parity**:
The guarantee that this package returns what R's **sampling** returns for the
same input. Where the two differ, the difference is deliberate and recorded.
_Avoid_: compatibility, fidelity, equivalence

**Golden**:
A recorded input/output pair generated by R's **sampling**, checked into the
repository and asserted against. A golden may also record that R *raises* on an
input.
_Avoid_: fixture, snapshot, reference vector, baseline

**Stream replay**:
Feeding a design the exact sequence of uniform random numbers R consumed, so a
Python draw can be compared to an R draw value-for-value rather than only in
distribution.
_Avoid_: seeding, RNG injection, deterministic mode
